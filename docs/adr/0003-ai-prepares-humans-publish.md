# AI prepares, humans publish

The AI may draft anything in Oppr Knowledge: Proposed revisions, merges, retirements, Training definitions, and Wiki Scopes through Guided creation. It may never publish or approve. Wikis and Attempt questions are the exception. They are generated output derived only from content that is already Published, so they need no approval of their own.

We chose this over letting the AI publish small fixes automatically. "Published = reviewed by a person" is what makes Ask, Wikis and Training records trustworthy, both under ISO 9001 and for a labour inspector. Losing that guarantee for convenience would undermine the product.
