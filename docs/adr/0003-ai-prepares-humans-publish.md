# AI prepares, humans publish

The AI may draft anything in Oppr Knowledge: Proposed revisions, merges, retirements, Training definitions, and Wiki Scopes through Guided creation. It may never publish or approve. Wikis and Attempt questions are the exception. They state only what is already Published, so they need no approval of their own: Wiki text is either generated from Published passages or phrased by an Engineer and verified claim by claim against them, with a second Engineer's confirm where code cannot prove the claim (docs/adr/0007).

We chose this over letting the AI publish small fixes automatically. "Published = reviewed by a person" is what makes Ask, Wikis and Training records trustworthy, both under ISO 9001 and for a labour inspector. Losing that guarantee for convenience would undermine the product.

Amended 2026-10-25 by docs/adr/0007 (people shape Wikis; Published Knowledge states them).
